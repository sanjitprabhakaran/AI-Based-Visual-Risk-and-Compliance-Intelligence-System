Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority. We are committed to
protecting your personal data.

Information We Collect

We collect the following personal information when you use Digital Twin AI:

  • Account data – username, email, password hash (securely stored).
  • Financial data – income, expenses, savings, transactions you choose to log.
  • Study data – study hours, subjects, productivity scores.
  • Fitness data – activity types, duration, calories burned.
  • Goals – descriptions and target dates.
  • Chat messages – questions and responses with the AI assistant.

How We Use Your Data

Your information helps Digital Twin AI provide the services you request, including:

  • Personalizing your dashboard and tracking the information you choose to record.
  • Generating financial, study, and fitness insights, forecasts, and simulations.
  • Responding to your questions and maintaining your account securely.

Data Security

We use safeguards designed to protect your account information. Passwords are stored as secure hashes. Please keep your login credentials private and contact us if you believe your account has been accessed without your permission.

Your Choices

You choose what financial, study, fitness, and goal information to enter. You may stop using the service at any time. AI-generated insights are provided to support your use of the application.
